## 📊 **Abstract**
Spatial aliasing remains the fundamental limitation in seismic acquisition. We introduce a revolutionary acquisition geometry based on the golden ratio (φ = 1.618...) that completely suppresses aliasing artifacts. Through 3D synthetic modeling, we demonstrate that a 60-sensor golden spiral array achieves:
- ✅ **Complete suppression** of spatial aliasing (0 dB ghost amplitude)
- ✅ **+16.3 dB improvement** in image signal-to-noise ratio
- ✅ **2.2× enhancement** in lateral resolution
- ✅ **6.7× better** weak target visibility
